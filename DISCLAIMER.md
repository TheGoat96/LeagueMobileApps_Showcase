## Positioning

This showcase documents solo, end-to-end mobile product work — concept, **realtime** platform, branding, web presence, marketing/ambassador programs, advertising opportunity design, client, cloud backend, and store release — as part of a multi-sport suite active roughly **2015–2019**. It demonstrates long-running mobile shipping and product-thinking experience, not a current consumer offering.
