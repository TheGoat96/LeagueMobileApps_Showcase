# Business & go-to-market (archival summary)

Portfolio summary of how League Mobile Apps was positioned commercially during its active years (~**2015–2019**). Sourced from the July 2016 pitch deck, business-opportunity deck, endorsement agreement template, and marketing creatives. **Not a live offering.**

The full July 2016 business plan is marked confidential and is **not** republished here. Signed ambassador contracts with personal information are also withheld; the unsigned endorsement template and pitch materials are included under [`receipts/`](./receipts).

---

## Product thesis: real-time connection

Every core feature was framed around **real-time** athlete engagement — not a static directory of courts:

| Real-time surface | What users felt instantly |
|-------------------|---------------------------|
| Live court occupancy | See how many players are checked in at a location right now |
| Location check-in pushes | Get notified when players arrive at bookmarked courts |
| Peer skill ratings | Rate / be rated on five fundamentals; stats update immediately |
| Rating notifications | Push when your overall rating or badge changes (`#GetYourStatsUp`) |
| Social rating badge | Share bragging rights to Facebook / Twitter / Instagram |
| In-app messages | Persistent inbox CTAs (check-in, create location, invite) |
| Rich / custom notifications | Brandable push with call-to-action destinations |
| Analytics | Interaction data to measure engagement with the app, peers, and affiliate brands |

Pitch competitive framing (2016): differentiate vs Nike Soccer, NBA, TLN, UrbanBallr, VolleyballUSA with **real-time ratings, stats, nearby features, location notifications, and rankings**.

## Marketing system (not an afterthought)

Go-to-market was designed as a multi-channel brand engine:

- **Athlete endorsements / brand ambassadors** — formal Endorsement Agreements (Persistence Multimedia Productions LLC) requiring profiles, check-ins, location creation, social rating posts, campaign hashtag `#GetYourStatsUp`, SMS download codes, and League gear photos
- **Social ads** — finished Facebook / Instagram / Twitter creatives per sport (2016)
- **SMS marketing** — short-code style download prompts in ambassador duties
- **Youth sports leagues** — sponsorship + grassroots targeting (ages 12–19 primary market)
- **App Store / Play distribution** — free suite titles across five sports
- **In-app messaging** — seasonal creatives driving check-in / create / invite behaviors

## Brand & advertising revenue potential

The commercial model tied **engagement loops** to **inventory**:

**Sales channels (pitch)**
- Mobile ad network(s)
- Custom notification ads
- Custom event campaigns

**Why realtime mattered commercially**  
Check-in, rating, and location notifications kept athletes opening the apps — creating frequency for ad placement. Geo + realtime also enabled **place/time contextual** inventory: sports businesses could reach athletes around tournaments, camps, tryouts, and known gathering spots via custom notification ads.

**High-level 2016 pitch targets (historical, not current forecasts)**
- ~22M U.S. youth-sports addressable market cited across five sports
- Ad RPM + custom notification ads as primary revenue levers
- Athlete endorsements and sports-marketing ops as primary growth spend

Secondary market called out in planning materials: local/national sports businesses (retail, teams, sports medicine, leagues/clubs) wanting to reach an active athlete audience.

## Admin ops console (campaign delivery)

The consumer apps were only half the platform. An internal **League Core Admin Site** let ops:

- Maintain the courts catalog (add / search / delete)
- Inspect players near a point or at a location
- Compose **text**, **rich**, and **in-app** campaigns
- Target **Location / Area / All**, with locale + device filters
- Attach sponsor / affiliate creative fields on rich notifications — the operational side of “custom notification ads”

## Brand ambassadors (executed)

This was not only a slide. In mid-2016 the company ran **micro-athlete endorsement** programs with signed agreements (templates + executed copies archived privately). Ambassador duties explicitly exercised the realtime product:

1. Create a user profile  
2. Check in at practices / facilities (≥10), creating missing locations when needed  
3. Share rating certificates to social (≥4) with `#GetYourStatsUp`  
4. Promote free download via SMS-style CTA  
5. Post photos in League gear  

Compensation mixed cash + in-kind League apparel — classic grassroots ambassador structure.
